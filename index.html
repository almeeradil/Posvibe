Frequently Asked Questions

How do invoice numbers work?

Numbering starts at 1 and increases automatically (2, 3, 4 ...). You can change the prefix or the next number in Settings.

My business name is not showing on the print.

Open Settings, fill in your business name, address, phone, NTN and save. Every print then uses that information.

Which barcode scanners are supported?

Any USB or Bluetooth scanner that types like a keyboard. Just focus the scan box and scan.

Is my data safe?

All records are stored on this device in your browser storage. Do not clear browser data without a backup.
